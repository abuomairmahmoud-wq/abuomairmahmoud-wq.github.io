Debt Snowball vs Avalanche: Which Pays Off Debt Faster?

If you have more than one debt, the two most popular payoff plans are the snowball and the avalanche. Both work the same way: pay the minimum on every debt, then put every extra dollar on one target debt. They differ only in which debt you target first.

Debt snowball: smallest balance first

You attack the debt with the smallest balance. You clear it quickly, which feels great and keeps you motivated. Then you roll that payment into the next smallest debt.

Debt avalanche: highest interest rate first

You attack the debt with the highest APR. Mathematically this usually costs less interest and can finish sooner, especially when a big credit card balance has a high rate.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose the avalanche if your rates are very different and you are disciplined.
  • Choose the snowball if you need quick wins to stay on track.
  • The best plan is the one you will stick with. Both are far better than paying only minimums.
