How to Make a Simple Monthly Budget in 15 Minutes (Free Template)

A budget is just a plan for your money before the month starts. You don't need an app or a finance degree. You need your take-home pay, a list of categories and 15 minutes.

Step 1: Start with take-home pay

Use the money that actually lands in your account after tax and deductions, not your salary before tax.

Step 2: List your fixed bills first

Rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance, phone and internet, debt payments. These are the same (or almost the same) every month.

Step 3: Give flexible spending a limit

Groceries, transport, eating out, subscriptions, gifts and fun money. Look at last month's bank statement for a realistic starting number.

Step 4: Pay yourself too

Add savings as a category, even if it is small. Treating savings like a bill is the easiest way to build an emergency fund.

Step 5: Track actual spending and adjust

Once a week, type what you really spent next to each budget. Any category in the red tells you where to cut back or where your budget was unrealistic. Adjust next month; a budget gets better every month you use it.
